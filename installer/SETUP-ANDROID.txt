POCKETDECK - turn an Android phone into a Stream Deck for a Windows PC
=====================================================================

The phone shows big buttons. Tapping one opens apps or folders on the PC, controls
Teams (mute, camera, share screen...), and controls music, with a now-playing notch.
The phone talks to the PC only through the USB cable or Android's "Wireless debugging".
Other devices on the network can't use it.

You need: a Windows 10/11 PC, an Android 11+ phone, and a USB data cable.


PART 1 - PC SETUP (about 3 minutes)
-----------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example, Downloads).
2. Double-click "Install PocketDeck.bat".
   - If Python is missing it installs it, or asks you to get it from python.org.
   - It downloads Google's adb tool, installs the music add-on, and puts a
     "USB Deck" shortcut on the desktop.
   - If Windows SmartScreen warns you, click "More info" > "Run anyway".
3. The USB Deck window opens on the PC. That's where you edit the buttons.


PART 2 - PHONE SETUP (one time)
-------------------------------
1. Turn on Developer options:
   Settings > About phone > Software information > tap "Build number" 7 times.
   (On Pixel and others: Settings > About phone > tap "Build number" 7 times.)
2. Settings > Developer options > turn ON "USB debugging".
3. Plug the phone into the PC with the cable. Unlock the phone and tap
   "Allow" (tick "Always allow from this computer").
4. On the phone, open Chrome and go to:      http://127.0.0.1:2346
   (type it exactly; it only works while the PC app is running and the phone is linked)
5. Chrome menu (three dots) > "Add to Home screen" > "Install".
   You now have a "USB Deck" app icon. Open it: the dot at the top turns green.


OPTIONAL - WITHOUT THE CABLE (Wireless debugging, same Wi-Fi)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Phone: Developer options > "Wireless debugging" > ON > tap the words
   "Wireless debugging" > "Pair device with pairing code".
2. PC: open a Command Prompt and run (use the numbers the phone shows):
      %USERPROFILE%\platform-tools\adb pair 192.168.x.x:PPPPP 123456
3. Done. At home the PC finds the phone by itself. On office networks that block
   discovery, type the "IP address & Port" from the Wireless debugging screen
   into the box at the top of the PC app and press Connect.
   (The port changes every time Wireless debugging is switched off and on.)


DAILY USE
---------
- Double-click "USB Deck" on the desktop (it starts everything), then open the
  USB Deck app on the phone.
- Edit buttons in the PC window: drag to reorder, click a tile to change its
  label, picture (real app icons) and what it does. Changes show on the phone
  within about 2 seconds.
- Phone: swipe between pages, the rotate button locks portrait/landscape, and
  the power button pauses the deck. While music plays, tap the black pill at
  the top for the full player.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- Phone says "Not connected": make sure the PC app is running, and that the
  cable is in (and the phone allowed USB debugging) or Wireless debugging is on.
- Phone shows "Check USB port": lint or moisture in the phone's port. Clean it
  gently with a wooden toothpick and try again.
- A button flashes red: the PC app's log (bottom of its window) says why.
- Teams buttons need the Teams meeting window open on the PC.
- Work laptops: IT may block installing Python or USB debugging.
